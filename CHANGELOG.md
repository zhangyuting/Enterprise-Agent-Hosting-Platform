# Changelog

All notable changes to the white paper are recorded here. Versions follow the
version number printed on the cover of the paper.

## 1.0 — 2026-09-24

First public release.

- English and Simplified Chinese editions, as PDF and as LaTeX source.
- Four parts: the case and the object; architecture and runtime contract;
  security, governance and runtime control; productization, delivery and
  evolution. Appendices: glossary, container contract quick reference,
  ten-invariant acceptance checklist.
- 24 figures, including a complete architecture view with a detail view for
  each region, and 32 tables.
